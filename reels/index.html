FAQ

Good to know

Do I have to film the content myself?+

No — send us your footage for editing, or book a full shoot in NYC.

What's the difference between editing and filming packages?+

With editing, you film and send us the raw footage. With filming, we handle the ideas, scripts, filming, and editing.

Can you help with ideas and scripts?+

Yes — ideas, hooks, and scripts are included in every filming package.

What if I already have raw footage?+

Perfect — pick an editing package and send it over.

What platforms are these made for?+

Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

How many revisions are included?+

Up to 3 rounds.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

No — pick whichever package fits what you need right now.

Can you film at my business?+

Yes — on-site filming is available across New York City.
